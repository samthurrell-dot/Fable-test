Bunker Billionaire (build 16)
Model: Claude Fable 5.2, default effort. Run in the Claude desktop app (Code tab), 13 Nov 2026.

The prompt, as Sam typed it:

  "There is a game design document for a game, bunker billionaire. Use the design doc and skills
  harvested from other builds to develop a high quality game prototype."

and, part way through, after the plan was to build the silo with the paper engines from build 15:

  "you can use the harvested skills in any way you want. Or not at all, the game design should be
  the primary driver"

The design document is "Bunker Billionaire: Act 2 design outline" (Sam Hurrell, 12 Nov 2026), a Word
file kept outside this repo. Its "first prototype" section asks for: phone-first HTML on one screen
(resources at the top, staff in the middle, today's event at the bottom); 6 staff, 4 resources,
5 tools, 3 promise types, 2 starting cards; 20 to 30 event cards; a 30-day run; two or three endings;
no server.

What was taken from the earlier builds and the skills in this repo:
  - the process from skills/showpiece-prompt-builder (a full v1, then rounds of naming the three
    weakest things; URL controls to reach exact moments; phone first; sound made in code, off until
    asked for; a five-line final reply);
  - the harvest clause (rules, content and screen kept apart; every tuning number a named constant;
    TECHNIQUES.md, ROUNDS.md, a demo page);
  - the sound recipe Sam approved on build 15 (event-tied noises and a small bell, silent at rest);
  - the cut-paper look of build 15, as flat SVG rather than its WebGL renderer.
What was not used: the three engines in knowledge/ (fold kinematics, card mesher, paper renderer).
A management game played a minute a day on a phone is better served by a light page.
