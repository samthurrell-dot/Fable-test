Bunker Billionaire (build 16)
Model: Claude Fable 5.2, default effort. Run in the Claude desktop app (Code tab), 13 Nov 2026.

The prompt, as Sam typed it:

  "There is a game design document for a game, bunker billionaire. Use the design doc and skills
  harvested from other builds to develop a high quality game prototype."

and, part way through, after the plan was to build the silo with the paper engines from build 15:

  "you can use the harvested skills in any way you want. Or not at all, the game design should be
  the primary driver"

The design document is "Bunker Billionaire: Act 2 design outline" (Sam Hurrell, 12 Nov 2026), a Word
file kept outside this repo. Its "first prototype" section asks for: phone-first HTML on one screen
(resources at the top, staff in the middle, today's event at the bottom); 6 staff, 4 resources,
5 tools, 3 promise types, 2 starting cards; 20 to 30 event cards; a 30-day run; two or three endings;
no server.

What was taken from the earlier builds and the skills in this repo:
  - the process from skills/showpiece-prompt-builder (a full v1, then rounds of naming the three
    weakest things; URL controls to reach exact moments; phone first; sound made in code, off until
    asked for; a five-line final reply);
  - the harvest clause (rules, content and screen kept apart; every tuning number a named constant;
    TECHNIQUES.md, ROUNDS.md, a demo page);
  - the sound recipe Sam approved on build 15 (event-tied noises and a small bell, silent at rest);
  - the cut-paper look of build 15, as flat SVG rather than its WebGL renderer.
What was not used: the three engines in knowledge/ (fold kinematics, card mesher, paper renderer).
A management game played a minute a day on a phone is better served by a light page.

Round 2 (14 Nov 2026), after Sam played v1. His words are quoted in full at the top of round 2 in
ROUNDS.md. In short: the picture was "incredibly basic" (use the paper engine); there was "not much
player agency" and "nothing really to manage or take care of"; being evil must be fun, with fewer
sensible options; and the start should let you choose your staff, each with pros and cons.
From round 2 the three engines in knowledge/ ARE used, copied unchanged into engine/.

Round 4 (16 to 17 Nov 2026), Sam's words: "have another run at the game, using the latest skills that have
been added to the folder/repo". The skills: skills/game-design-principles, skills/choice-and-systems-design,
skills/game-design-review (added 16 Nov 2026 with a review of this build in ROUNDS.md). Round 4 took the
review's three weakest things as its brief. Started on Fable 5.2, finished on Opus 5.5.

Round 5 (21 Nov 2026), after Sam played round 4 to day 3: "the different mechanics or levels just aren't
clear so I'm not sure what the objective is, and there aren't any upgrade paths ... the actions I can take
are either placid obvious good decisions or cruel decisions, there isn't that funny factor I was thinking
about. I only got to day 3 and was already quite disengaged." Run on Opus 5.5.

Round 6 (21 Nov 2026), after Sam saw the milestone deck: "alongside the projects there needs to be a push and pull
with requests or promises, these need to cost resources or need attention over a series of days and have a meaningful
consequence for completing it or not". Run on Opus 5.5.

Round 7 (21 Nov 2026), after Sam played round 6: the Solemn Promise gave no reason to play it; "a mandatory starting
project to start with would be compelling and also introduce some resource scarcity"; "From the start I can basically
do nothing and survive to day 30"; "The project still doesn't require anything of me, I just need to wait"; and the
request writing did not feel tight. Run on Opus 5.5.

Round 8 (21 Nov 2026), after Sam played round 7: the You sheet's writing "just doesn't make any sense"; "On day 11
Ilse left but I couldn't see any reason why. I wasn't getting any indication that they were about to leave or that
they didn't like me." Run on Opus 5.5.

Round 9 (21 Nov 2026), during round 8: "I'm now running low on water but haven't come across any way to reduce it
sufficiently or to increase the water supplies". Run on Opus 5.5.
