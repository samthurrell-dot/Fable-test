# Bunker Billionaire: design notes

The working design for the prototype, kept as `skills/choice-and-systems-design` asks: the spine, the resources and an audit of every decision in a day. Sam's own outline ("Bunker Billionaire: Act 2", a Word file outside the repo) is the source; this records what the build does with it. Updated 21 Nov 2026, round 7. (The decision audit below is from round 4, with requests added in round 6; actions are now cards, see ROUNDS.md.)

## Spine

1. **Feeling:** the guilty, funny pleasure of living absurdly well at everyone else's expense, and the creeping unease of not knowing who is about to stop you.
2. **Question:** how lavishly can you live, and what will you do to the people below you to keep it up?
3. **Loop:** read the morning, settle one piece of business, answer a request or do today's part of what you promised, push the project (materials, overtime or your own time), play two of four cards, set your lifestyle and their rations, lights out. Day 1 also chooses the first project.
4. **Run shape:** 30 days. Starts with how you got the bunker, who you let in at the hatch and a management philosophy; builds through projects and a growing deck; ends with your score and title, or with you removed, and a reckoning of who paid.
5. **Goal and score:** opulence, with five titles. Still being in charge on day 30 is what makes the score count.
6. **Cut line:** no research tree, no combat screen, no exact loyalty numbers, no factions with names, no bugged rooms or planted spies, no world beyond the radio and the hatch. (Round 5 cut comfort, five standing orders, requests and informants.)

## Resources

| Resource | What fills it | What drains it | At zero |
|---|---|---|---|
| Food | the farm each night; searching the bunks; some cards | staff rations, your table, sabotage, broken fridge or lights | a crisis card, then everyone dies after three nights |
| Water | the recycling plant each night | drinking, crops, your hot tub, a broken pump | as food |
| Power | the generator, as well as the engineer runs it | heating, your cinema, cameras, a broken generator | as food |
| Medicine | a few cards | accidents, cold staff levels, a broken medicine fridge | the doctor cannot treat anyone; cards get worse |
| Luxury | a few cards; searching the bunks | perks, informants, repairs, treats for yourself, wages | nothing left to bribe with or to mend with; fear fades faster |
| Opulence (your score) | lifestyle each night, projects, indulgent and ruthless cards, some event choices | open-door talks, some event choices | nothing: it is the score, and cannot go below zero |

Hidden: each person's loyalty, fear and step on the threat ladder; the bunker's unrest; the slope (shown, not hidden: how far down you are).

## Decision audit (a typical day)

| Decision | Trade-off | Depends on the situation? | Lasting? | Shown before | Shown after | Dominant option? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Today's event card | stores against loyalty, comfort against fairness | yes (who sent it, what is short) | often | open costs on each button | result text and chips | no (bench: careful player weighs by what is short) |
| Standing orders (7 dials) | stores against loyalty against your comfort | yes (what will run out) | every night until changed | tonight's cost per store, in the bar | the bar's forecast changes at once | no; the right setting moves with the stores |
| A request from one person (round 6) | days of stores or card plays against a lasting perk, with a fallout if you fail; or a selfish third answer; or refuse | yes (who asks, what is short, what is already promised, the project under way) | yes: a perk for good, or a fallout and a step towards removing you | the cost of each part, how many parts and days, what keeping and breaking do | the pips, the morning report, the ledger, their file | no: the bench keeps about 5 of 8 and refuses or sidesteps the rest |
| A broken machine | your luxury against everyone's goodwill; leaving it costs a store a night | yes (how much luxury, how unhappy they are) | until mended | the drain a night | who broke it, if you paid to mend it properly | no |
| An action: kind (talk, perk, test, informant, speech, promise) | comfort or luxury for loyalty or knowledge | yes | yes | costs and gains on the button | text, faces, files | worth less the further down the slope you are |
| An action: a measure on the slope | someone's loyalty (and their friend's) for fear, comfort and quiet | yes (which steps are open, who is dangerous) | yes, and it opens the next step | comfort gained on the button | text, faces, the slope, the end screen | no: the tyrant and the saint both win about as often on the bench |

Count on a phone: one card, up to two waiting things, up to three actions. Bench (careful player, round 4): about 130 words to read and 2.6 decisions a day.
